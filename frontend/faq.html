FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about the MediScan AI experience.

Does MediScan AI require an account?

No. The frontend is intentionally designed without login, signup, or authentication.

Are prices guaranteed?

No. Prices and availability can change. Users should verify the final price and stock with the pharmacy before purchasing.

What is the AI Summary?

It is a concise explanation of the comparison results, such as the lowest price, potential savings, and availability.

Will it work without a backend?

The frontend pages and demo comparison UI work without a backend. Live medicine data requires the team's API endpoint.

Can I use dark mode?

Yes. The theme switch is available in the header and is remembered between pages.

What should I verify before buying?

Always verify medicine name, strength, pack size, final price, availability, and pharmacy details before purchasing.
